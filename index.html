Our Story

Why Your BCA Notes Exists

Built to solve a real problem faced by every BCA student — scattered notes, lost messages, and chaotic learning.

The Problem We Solve

In college groups, teachers share notes lecture-by-lecture. Chats get messy, messages get deleted, and students lose access to their study material.

Our Solution

Your BCA Notes is a simple, open-source platform where every BCA student can find semester-wise notes, YouTube video lectures, GeeksforGeeks articles, and official roadmaps — all in sequential order, searchable, and free forever.

  • ✓ Complete BCA syllabus aligned across all semesters
  • ✓ Semester → Subject → Module → Topic structure
  • ✓ YouTube video links for every module
  • ✓ GeeksforGeeks articles for most modules
  • ✓ Roadmap.sh links for career guidance
  • ✓ No login, no ads, no paywalls
